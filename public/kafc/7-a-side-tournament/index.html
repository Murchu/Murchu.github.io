Seasonal programme

The Sevens return each summer

The tournament runs from June into early July each year, with competitions for underage teams, senior men, senior ladies and Over 35s. Killarney Credit Union continues as the tournament’s main sponsor.

The 2026 tournament has concluded. This page will be updated with the next tournament’s dates and entry details.
